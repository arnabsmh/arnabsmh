### Research

I am a doctoral researcher in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at Rajiv Gandhi University. My work focuses on **speech technology and NLP for low-resource languages of North East India**, with current research centered on:

- **Assamese Speech Technology:** Automatic speech recognition (ASR), acoustic modeling, and cross-lingual transfer learning.
- **Galo Text & NLP:** Computational language resources and text processing for indigenous languages.
- **AI & Cybersecurity:** Deepfake/synthetic speech detection, machine learning for threat detection, and digital forensics.

Previously, I was an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at **Darrang College (Autonomous)**, Tezpur, Assam.
